Questions

How do I order from this website?

Add pieces to your bag and tap Send order on WhatsApp. Your order reaches our chat with names and prices filled in, and we confirm the rest.

Do you ship outside Katni?

Yes. We ship across India and abroad. We tell you the shipping cost before you pay.

Will the jewellery tarnish?

Our pieces are made to be waterproof, anti-tarnish and skin-friendly for everyday wear. Ask us for care tips when you order.

Can I see a piece before I buy it?

Yes. Visit the store, or ask on WhatsApp for a short video of any piece.

Can you make a bouquet or hamper for my budget?

Yes. Build a hamper on the gifts page, or message us your budget and the occasion.

How can I pay?

In the store you can pay by UPI or cash. For delivery orders we share payment details on WhatsApp.
